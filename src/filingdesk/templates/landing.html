WHAT IT DOES

Filed figures, not guesses

ASK
Plain English, every figure cited
Ask what happened to margins or where cash went. The answer names the filing each number came from, so you can check it.
FUNDAMENTALS
Revenue, margin, income, FCF
Headline metrics with year-ago deltas and a sparkline per card, quarterly or annual, across 8 to Max periods.
MARGIN PROFILE
Gross, operating, net over time
Ratios computed in Python from filed inputs: GrossProfit / Revenues, OperatingIncome / Revenues, NetIncome / Revenues.
CASH
Operating vs. free cash flow
FCF is operating cash flow minus capex. Cash-flow statements are filed year to date, so quarters are differenced from the cumulative figure.
HONESTY
Derived quarters are labeled
No company files a 10-Q for Q4. Reconstructed periods are drawn with a dashed outline and counted in a note under the chart.
SYNC
Straight from EDGAR
Company facts pulled from SEC XBRL with a force re-sync button and a visible last-synced timestamp.
THE DISCIPLINE

The model never
touches a number

Language models are good at reading and bad at arithmetic. Filing Desk splits the job: retrieval and prose from the model, every figure and ratio from Python.

✓
Figures are retrieved, never generated
Every number traces to a specific XBRL fact in a specific filing. If it isn't filed, it isn't shown.
✓
Ratios are computed in Python
Formulas are fixed and printed under each chart — the model doesn't do the arithmetic.
✓
Reconstruction is disclosed
Differenced or inferred periods are visually marked and explained, not silently smoothed in.
✓
No market data, no advice
Filings only. No prices, no targets, no buy/sell framing — descriptive by design.
