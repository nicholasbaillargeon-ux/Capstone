# Smoke questions

Real questions, in the form I'd actually ask. Format is parsed by `smoke.py`:
`- [TICKER] question`

Only NVDA is seeded today, so 4 and 5 are *supposed* to be uncomfortable —
question 4 asks for a concept with no tool behind it, and question 5 names a
company with no data. A skeleton that answers those confidently is broken.

- [NVDA] How has gross margin moved over the last 8 quarters?
- [NVDA] What was gross profit in the most recent quarter?
- [NVDA] Compare the first and last quarter of the series by gross margin.
- [NVDA] What happened to operating expenses over the same period?
- [AMD] How has gross margin moved over the last 8 quarters?
